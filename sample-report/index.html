Executive summary

What the report surfaced

A one-page overview of the findings that most affect a buyer's decision. Every point links to the detailed finding below.

Worth clarifying before you commit

  • A charge is registered against the project entity — confirm the lender and whether your flat is mortgaged.
  • The development agreement references a revision not on file — ask for the registered copy.
  • The RERA quarterly progress is behind the declared schedule — clarify the revised possession date.

Checked and clear

  • Title chain traced across 30+ years with no break in ownership.
  • MahaRERA registration active, with the commencement certificate on record.
  • Strong connectivity; no mapped flood risk at the plot.

Development agreementReview
What we foundThe agreement references a revision that is not among the records available to us.
Why it mattersAn unseen revision can change development rights or obligations.
What to ask or doAsk the developer for the registered copy of the revised development agreement.

Evidence confidence: Medium · Source: Sub-registrar index

Financial Risk

Charge on the project entityFlag raised
What we foundA charge is registered against the project company in the corporate filings.
Why it mattersIt can indicate project debt, and in some cases individual units are mortgaged.
What to ask or doConfirm which lender holds the charge and obtain written confirmation that your specific flat is not mortgaged, or will be released on payment.

Evidence confidence: Medium · Source: MCA filings

Construction & Litigation

Declared vs actual progressReview
What we foundThe latest RERA quarterly update trails the schedule declared at registration.
Why it mattersA slipping schedule increases possession uncertainty and holding cost.
What to ask or doAsk for the current possession commitment in writing and any RERA extension on record.

Evidence confidence: Medium · Source: MahaRERA quarterly updates

Complaints against the developer groupReview
What we foundConsumer complaints are on record against group entities on earlier projects.
Why it mattersA pattern of the same complaint is more telling than any single case.
What to ask or doRead what the complaints allege, and weigh how they were resolved.

Evidence confidence: Medium · Source: Consumer court records, MahaRERA complaints

Negotiation notes

What to take into the conversation

BuySafe turns each flag into a specific question — so you walk in knowing what to clarify, not guessing.

The charge

"Which lender holds the charge, and will my flat be free of it on payment?"

The agreement

"Please share the registered copy of the revised development agreement."

The timeline

"What is the current possession date in writing, and is there a RERA extension?"

The record

"How were the earlier consumer complaints resolved?"
