The methodology

How BuySafe checks a property

BuySafe is independent, buyer-side research. We work from public records — not the builder's brochure — and turn what we find into one evidence report you can act on. This page explains exactly what we check, the evidence we use, and what the report does and does not cover.

What we check

Six areas of risk, checked on every report

These are the areas a buyer should have independently checked before committing. Not every check is available for every property — where a record cannot be found, we say so rather than infer.

Area 01

Legal & Title

Who owns the land, whether the title is clear, and whether the records support the project.

  • Land title clarity
  • Ownership verification
  • Encumbrance / mortgage
  • Litigation / court cases
  • Development agreement
  • Search report validation
  • NA order verification
  • Land reservation issues

Area 02

Approvals & Compliance

Whether the project holds the approvals and regulatory status expected for its stage.

  • Commencement Certificate (CC)
  • IOD approval
  • Approved plans
  • Environmental clearance
  • Fire NOC
  • Airport NOC
  • Municipal approvals
  • RERA compliance status

Area 03

Builder & Reputation

The developer's execution history, beyond the brochure.

  • Past project history
  • Delivery track record
  • Delay history
  • Litigation history
  • Financial distress indicators
  • Customer complaints
  • Group company analysis

Area 04

Financial Risk

The available evidence on project funding, debt and financial risk.

  • Project funding status
  • Bank approval status
  • Mortgage disclosures
  • Debt exposure
  • Cash-flow risk indicators

Area 05

Construction Progress

What the available evidence shows about actual progress versus what was promised.

  • Actual vs promised progress
  • Site verification (where in scope)
  • Construction stage mapping
  • Labour / work activity
  • Delay probability analysis

Area 06

Litigation & Disputes

Disputes that could affect the project, the developer, or your decision.

  • Civil court cases
  • RERA complaints
  • NCLT matters
  • Consumer court matters
  • Land disputes
  • Environmental disputes

What BuySafe does not cover

Clear about our limits

  • BuySafe is independent research, not legal, structural, valuation or investment advice.
  • Findings reflect the public record as at the date of checking; facts can change afterwards.
  • Some records may be unavailable, incomplete or contradicted by another source — we say so rather than infer.
  • BuySafe is not a law firm, a registered valuer, a structural engineer, a SEBI-registered adviser, or a government authority.
  • BuySafe does not decide the purchase for you. It gives you the evidence to decide.
